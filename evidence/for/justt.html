Agent dispute evidence

A constructed agent card dispute, and the missing evidence

Read this first

Prepared for the Justt team. As of 2026-10-26, Justt has not reviewed or endorsed this page, and no page or menu on this site links to it.

The case on this page is a constructed test case. A script built the signed log with a test key; only the witness receipts are live, signed by Headless Oracle's witness on 25 October 2026; and each card charge time is a test value, the receipt's time plus 60 seconds written to the whole second, so the check that the witness receipt's time is before the charge time passes by construction. No real order was placed, no card was charged, and no AI agent or gate ran. The limits below are the verifier's standard wording for a log of this format; here Headless Oracle holds the test key and a script built the log. The case page linked below says how it was built.

The example's own limit:

Headless Oracle is the gate's operator and the witness; the traveller, the hotel and the charge are test parties; test card data only.

limittext
L24v0.10. For a card payment, the payment proof is the card network's authorization record, which the issuer holds; this bundle neither carries nor verifies it. What this verdict shows is whether the decision and the order the gate recorded were witnessed before the charge's authorization time as stated, and whether that order matches the stated charge: the stated charge and its time are the statements of the person running the verifier, taken from the processor's records.
L14Headless Oracle operates both the gate whose key signed this log and the witness, so the log and the witness receipt are statements by one party.
L3The witness receipt is Headless Oracle's signed statement that at received_at it was shown a checkpoint signed by the key with that thumbprint; it is only as reliable as Headless Oracle and its signing key. The witness is operated by Headless Oracle, which also publishes the gate software: it is independent of an operator other than Headless Oracle, not of Headless Oracle.

The missing evidence

The four "missing evidence" items in Ronen Shnidman, "Agentic Commerce Chargebacks and Solving the Evidence Gap" (https://justt.ai/blog/solving-agentic-commerce-chargebacks/, read 2026-10-26), each with what this bundle shows and the state in variant 1's online verdict:

itemwhat this bundle showsstate in we-5-1
"Proof that the customer delegated authority"The customer's signed instruction.

L12: The principal key in a minimal instruction is bound to no person, account or payer; the bundle shows that the holder of that key signed the instruction.

B-INS BOUND
"Timing and scope of approval"The instruction's validity window and card limits, and the witness time against the stated authorization.

L7: Times on the principal's, the issuer's, the operator's and Headless Oracle's clocks are those parties' statements, and the block timestamp is set by the block producer within the chain's rules. Only the orderings that need no clock rest on signatures and hashes alone.

B-INS BOUND; B-WIT BOUND; agent_decision_before_auth true
"Rules or parameters that were set"The gate's decision on the action (decision: allow) and the instruction's card limits as the verdict lists them (currency inside, max_unit_price_minor inside, categories inside, refundable_only inside, service_window inside, locations inside). Gap: a chirindo record does not commit to the policy that produced the decision.B-INS BOUND
"Did the agent correctly interpret the instructions"Not shown by the records in this bundle.

L1: A gate entry is the operator's own signed statement of what its gate saw and decided. The bundle does not show that a decision was wise, that the agent read the instruction correctly, or that any member's contents are true.
