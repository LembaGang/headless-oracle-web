Agent dispute evidence

A hotel booking made by an AI agent, and what the evidence shows

What this example does not show

The example's own limit, as its page file states it:

Headless Oracle is the gate's operator and the witness; the traveller, the hotel and the charge are test parties; test card data only.

Three limits from the verifier's online verdict:

limittext
L24v0.10. For a card payment, the payment proof is the card network's authorization record, which the issuer holds; this bundle neither carries nor verifies it. What this verdict shows is whether the decision and the order the gate recorded were witnessed before the charge's authorization time as stated, and whether that order matches the stated charge: the stated charge and its time are the statements of the person running the verifier, taken from the processor's records.
L14Headless Oracle operates both the gate whose key signed this log and the witness, so the log and the witness receipt are statements by one party.
L3The witness receipt is Headless Oracle's signed statement that at received_at it was shown a checkpoint signed by the key with that thumbprint; it is only as reliable as Headless Oracle and its signing key. The witness is operated by Headless Oracle, which also publishes the gate software: it is independent of an operator other than Headless Oracle, not of Headless Oracle.

The case

An AI agent books a hotel stay for a customer. The customer has signed an instruction that sets limits on what the agent may book. The agent's call passes a gate, which records its decision in a signed log, and Headless Oracle's witness signs a receipt for that log's checkpoint. Then a test card charge is made.

The customer, the hotel, the stay, its dates and the charge are test values made for this example. Nothing here is a real booking.

Variant 1 stays inside the instruction. Variant 2 books a non-refundable rate, and the instruction allows only refundable ones.

Every limit of the online verdict

limittext
L1A gate entry is the operator's own signed statement of what its gate saw and decided. The bundle does not show that a decision was wise, that the agent read the instruction correctly, or that any member's contents are true.
L2A log records what passed through the gate. It does not show that no action bypassed the gate. The verifier reports the gate version each entry names; chirindo entries through release 0.5.2 carry the constant 0.0.1 in place of a version. chirindo through release 0.5.2 (source read at tags v0.3.0, v0.4.0, v0.5.1 and v0.5.2; run on 0.5.2) forwarded a tools/call without an id member without evaluating policy or writing a receipt, and ran an allowed tools/call with id null without a receipt.
L3The witness receipt is Headless Oracle's signed statement that at received_at it was shown a checkpoint signed by the key with that thumbprint; it is only as reliable as Headless Oracle and its signing key. The witness is operated by Headless Oracle, which also publishes the gate software: it is independent of an operator other than Headless Oracle, not of Headless Oracle.
L6A key's binding to an organisation goes no further than an https origin that served it to the verifier (B-KEY BOUND) or, for a standalone key, than the key itself (KEY-ONLY). An address is an address, not a person or a company.
L7Times on the principal's, the issuer's, the operator's and Headless Oracle's clocks are those parties' statements, and the block timestamp is set by the block producer within the chain's rules. Only the orderings that need no clock rest on signatures and hashes alone.
L8This bundle is named in none of the card network rules read for this specification (scope: the pages its sources list; the dispute chapters of the Visa Core Rules and the body of the Mastercard Chargeback Guide could not be read). It is evidence a decider may weigh within the existing reason codes.
L9The bundle carries what its assembler chose to put in. A verifier can show that the pieces present bind; it cannot show that a piece which would contradict them was left out, except where the witness's receipts show a longer history than the log carried (TRUNCATED) or a different one (FAIL).
L12The principal key in a minimal instruction is bound to no person, account or payer; the bundle shows that the holder of that key signed the instruction.
L13Uses and budget are counted only over what the bundle carries: the records whose arguments it carries and the payments it binds. Uses and payments outside the bundle cannot be counted; the output gives the number of records whose arguments are not carried.
L14Headless Oracle operates both the gate whose key signed this log and the witness, so the log and the witness receipt are statements by one party.
L17This version does not verify card payments: whether the action connects to the card payment is not verified.
L24v0.10. For a card payment, the payment proof is the card network's authorization record, which the issuer holds; this bundle neither carries nor verifies it. What this verdict shows is whether the decision and the order the gate recorded were witnessed before the charge's authorization time as stated, and whether that order matches the stated charge: the stated charge and its time are the statements of the person running the verifier, taken from the processor's records.
L25v0.10. The committed order is the agent's statement at decision time, as the gate recorded it: its merchant, items, prices, refundability, dates and places are what the agent's call named. The bundle does not show that the merchant fulfilled that order, that the charge was for it beyond the fields compared, or that an item's attributes are true.

Run it yourself

git clone https://github.com/LembaGang/chirindo.git
cd chirindo
git checkout 6285832c294493001b12d4467259c04a2c4890fb
cd examples/ordering-evidence-h4
npm ci
cd ../evidence-bundle
npm ci
node check-bundle.mjs pages/we-5-1.bundle.json --charge-id TEST-ch-we5-1 --charge-amount 28000 --charge-currency EUR --charge-authorized-at 2026-10-25T15:18:03Z --merchant-order-id TEST-H-2001 --pins pages/we-5-1-pins > we-5-1.verdict.json
node check-bundle.mjs pages/we-5-1.bundle.json --charge-id TEST-ch-we5-1 --charge-amount 28000 --charge-currency EUR --charge-authorized-at 2026-10-25T15:18:03Z --merchant-order-id TEST-H-2001 --pins pages/we-5-1-pins --online > we-5-1.online-verdict.json
node make-exhibit.mjs we-5-1.online-verdict.json --page letter --out we-5-1.online.letter.pdf
node make-datapoints.mjs we-5-1.online-verdict.json > we-5-1.online.datapoints.json

Or download the zip below, extract it, and start from cd chirindo-evidence/examples/ordering-evidence-h4. The charge and its authorization time are test values. In a real dispute, the person running the verifier states them from the dispute record they hold.
