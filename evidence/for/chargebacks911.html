Agent dispute evidence

A constructed agent card dispute, and the records behind it

Read this first

Prepared for the Chargebacks911 team. Chargebacks911 has not reviewed or endorsed this page. It is not listed on this site.

The case on this page is a constructed test case. A script built the signed log with a test key; only the witness receipts are live, signed by Headless Oracle's witness on 25 October 2026; and each card charge time is a test value, the receipt's time plus 60 seconds written to the whole second, so the check that the witness receipt's time is before the charge time passes by construction. No real order was placed, no card was charged, and no AI agent or gate ran. The limits below are the verifier's standard wording for a log of this format; here Headless Oracle holds the test key and a script built the log. The case page linked below says how it was built.

The example's own limit:

Headless Oracle is the gate's operator and the witness; the traveller, the hotel and the charge are test parties; test card data only.

limittext
L24v0.10. For a card payment, the payment proof is the card network's authorization record, which the issuer holds; this bundle neither carries nor verifies it. What this verdict shows is whether the decision and the order the gate recorded were witnessed before the charge's authorization time as stated, and whether that order matches the stated charge: the stated charge and its time are the statements of the person running the verifier, taken from the processor's records.
L14Headless Oracle operates both the gate whose key signed this log and the witness, so the log and the witness receipt are statements by one party.
L3The witness receipt is Headless Oracle's signed statement that at received_at it was shown a checkpoint signed by the key with that thumbprint; it is only as reliable as Headless Oracle and its signing key. The witness is operated by Headless Oracle, which also publishes the gate software: it is independent of an operator other than Headless Oracle, not of Headless Oracle.

Their list, and this bundle

As reported by The Paypers on 2026-05-15 (Iulia Musat, "Chargebacks911 warns of dispute risk gap in agentic payments" (https://thepaypers.com/fraud-and-fincrime/news/chargebacks911-warns-of-dispute-risk-gap-in-agentic-payments, read 2026-11-04)), which paraphrases the company: the first three are the steps Chargebacks911 recommends, and the fourth is among the records the article says the company's own dispute system captures. So the instruction was on record before the charge, not assembled after a dispute. The moment of delegation itself is the principal's own statement.

L2: A log records what passed through the gate. It does not show that no action bypassed the gate. The verifier reports the gate version each entry names; chirindo entries through release 0.5.2 carry the constant 0.0.1 in place of a version. chirindo through release 0.5.2 (source read at tags v0.3.0, v0.4.0, v0.5.1 and v0.5.2; run on 0.5.2) forwarded a tools/call without an id member without evaluating policy or writing a receipt, and ran an allowed tools/call with id null without a receipt.

L9: The bundle carries what its assembler chose to put in. A verifier can show that the pieces present bind; it cannot show that a piece which would contradict them was left out, except where the witness's receipts show a longer history than the log carried (TRUNCATED) or a different one (FAIL).

The indemnification launch

On 2026-10-27 The Paypers reported the launch of "an AI agent purchase indemnification service designed to protect merchants against qualifying chargebacks arising from these transactions", and printed: "Donald Kossmann, CTO of Chargebacks911, said that in agentic commerce the transaction is only one part of the picture, and that understanding behaviour, authorisation, and what happens after the purchase is necessary to assess risk." (Claudia Pincovski, "Chargebacks911 launches indemnification for AI agent purchases" (https://thepaypers.com/fraud-and-fincrime/news/chargebacks911-launches-indemnification-for-ai-agent-purchases, read 2026-11-04)).
